Important Security Notice

sales@amdmachines.com and every other email address on the amdmachines.com domain are potentially monitored and administered by Mexican nationals. Sending ITAR controlled technical data to any of these addresses may constitute an export to a foreign person.

For any ITAR project, send no material of any kind until AMD Machines has confirmed the destination address in writing.

ITAR Inquiries

AMD Machines has no designated ITAR contact point, and no address, form, or upload on this website is approved for ITAR controlled information.

If a project may involve ITAR controlled information, notify AMD Machines that the project may be ITAR sensitive. Do not describe the controlled content. Provide only your name, your company, a telephone number, and that statement. AMD Machines will then respond with the applicable guideline for how the information must be handled and identify what channel, if any, may be used.

Send no drawings, models, specifications, or other material until AMD Machines has confirmed the destination address in writing.
